Bubble Waffles, Crepes & Desserts

Tastea Boba isn’t only a tea cup. The dessert counter runs bubble waffles and fresh crepes alongside the teas — the combo Appleton was missing.

Full menu Call (920) 572-8191

The bubble waffle

Batter piped into its bubble grid, cooked crisp on the outside, soft inside — the ice-cream-shop waffle you order next to your milk tea, not after it.

Crepes, folded to order

Sweet fillings, savory folds — the press called them out beside the smoothies and slushies as part of what the counter makes fresh.

And the rest of the case

The shop serves other foods and desserts beyond tea, waffles and crepes — the counter keeps its own rotation, so ask what’s on today, or call ahead.
