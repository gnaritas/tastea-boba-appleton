A new dessert counter on Calumet Street

Tastea Boba opened in the former Little Caesars building at 317 E Calumet St, bringing Appleton its own tea-and-dessert counter: tea shaken with milk or cut with fruit, waffles piped into bubbles, crepes folded fresh.

Find the bright counter, place your order, and give it a second at one of the tables. Rated 4.6 by Google reviewers already — come see why.
