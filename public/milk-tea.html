Milk Tea & Fruit Tea in Appleton

At Tastea Boba, tea is the house craft: milk tea shaken smooth, fruit tea shaken bright. Flavors on the board include taro, thai, passion fruit and lychee — plus the rest of what the counter pours.

Call to order ahead See the full board

Classics, shaken to order

Taro milk tea is the one people come back for; thai tea brings the orange, aromatic shake. If you like it lighter, the fruit teas cut the sweetness — passion fruit and lychee lead the list the press wrote about, and the board has more.

Smoothies and slushies sit on the same counter when you want the fruit whole — blended, frosted, made after you order.

With your waffle

A milk tea travels well next to a bubble waffle or a folded crepe — the dessert counter is two steps from the tea station. See the waffle & crepe page.
